Professional teeth whitening: what to expect

Shop-bought strips and kits can only do so much. In-clinic whitening uses stronger, safer, dentist-controlled products and gives a visibly lighter result in one appointment.

We begin with a cleaning, because whitening works best on a clean surface and gels should never be applied over untreated cavities or inflamed gums. Gums are protected, the whitening agent is applied and activated in short cycles, and we finish with a fluoride application to calm sensitivity.

  • Noticeable results. Several shades lighter than over-the-counter kits.
  • Enamel protected. Every step is supervised by a dentist.
  • Sensitivity plan. Desensitising gel and a tailored protocol for sensitive teeth.
  • Aftercare. Simple guidance on coffee, tea, wine and tobacco to make results last.

When whitening may not be the right choice

Whitening does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, and darkened teeth after trauma or root canal treatment respond poorly. White spots from fluorosis can improve, but results vary.

In these cases we suggest honest alternatives: targeted stain removal, porcelain veneers or new zirconia crowns matched to your new shade. We would rather tell you now than sell you a treatment that disappoints.

How to remove tea and coffee stains from teeth

Surface stains come from tannins, pigments and tobacco that sit in the microscopic grooves of your enamel. Brushing harder does not help and can wear enamel down. A professional air polish removes the pigment in minutes, without scraping, and is the first step before whitening if you want the brightest result.

Bleeding gums? Deep periodontal cleaning explained

Gums that bleed when you brush are a warning sign, not normal. Tartar below the gum line feeds bacteria that inflame the gums, and over time can damage the bone that holds your teeth. A deep periodontal cleaning works in four stages:

  • Subgingival scaling. Hard tartar is removed from below the gum line.
  • Pocket debridement. Periodontal pockets around each tooth are cleaned.
  • Root planing. Root surfaces are smoothed so gums can reattach.
  • Bacterial control. Inflammation settles and harmful bacteria are reduced.

This non-surgical treatment stops gum disease from progressing, helps prevent loose teeth and bad breath, and is far cheaper than treating advanced bone loss later with oral surgery and bone grafting.

Frequently asked questions

How much does teeth whitening cost in Tirana, Albania?

Professional teeth whitening at Emerald Dental Clinic in Tirana costs €120, compared with roughly $600–$1,200 in the USA and £500–£1,000 in the UK. The dental check-up and consultation beforehand are free.

How much does a deep teeth cleaning cost in Albania?

A deep dental cleaning costs €30 per session at Emerald Dental in Tirana. A deep periodontal cleaning (curettage) costs €60, air polish costs €40 and a fluoride treatment costs €30.

How often should I have my teeth professionally cleaned?

Most people should have a professional teeth cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease, bleeding gums or wear braces, your dentist may recommend visits every three to four months.

Is professional teeth whitening safe for enamel?

Yes. When carried out by a dentist with professional-grade products, whitening is safe for healthy enamel. The dentist checks for cavities and gum problems first and adjusts the protocol for sensitive teeth.

How do you remove tea and coffee stains from teeth?

The fastest way to remove tea, coffee and smoking stains is an air polish treatment, which uses a jet of air, water and fine powder to lift surface stains without scraping the enamel. Whitening can then lighten the natural tooth colour.

Can teeth whitening be done on crowns, veneers or fillings?

No. Whitening only changes natural tooth enamel, so crowns, veneers and fillings keep their colour. If you plan to replace them, whiten first so the dentist can match the new restoration to your brighter shade.

Does deep teeth cleaning hurt?

A standard cleaning is generally painless. A deep periodontal cleaning below the gum line is done with local anaesthetic when needed, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Gums can be tender for a day or two.

Can I combine a dental cleaning with a holiday in Tirana?

Yes. Check-up, cleaning, air polish and whitening can often be completed in a single visit, so a short trip to Tirana is enough. Treatments that need more visits, such as implants, are planned with your dentist in advance.
