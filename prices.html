Temporary Crown (Composite/Acrylic)

Temporary crowns made of composite or acrylic materials are used to protect the implant site and maintain aesthetics during the healing phase after implant placement. These crowns are placed shortly after the implant procedure and help preserve gum contour, support surrounding teeth, and allow the patient to eat and speak comfortably.

Read more

Although not as durable as permanent crowns, they are essential for ensuring a smooth transition during osseointegration, the process in which the implant fuses with the jawbone. Typically, permanent crowns are placed approximately six months after the implant to ensure optimal stability and long-term success.

Composite Veneer

Composite veneers are thin layers of tooth-colored resin applied to the front surface of teeth to improve their appearance and restore function. These veneers can be placed in a single visit, making them a convenient and cost-effective solution for correcting issues such as discoloration, minor chips, gaps, or slight misalignment.

Read more

They enhance the natural aesthetics of the smile while preserving more of the tooth structure compared to other restorative options. Although not as long-lasting or stain-resistant as porcelain veneers, composite veneers provide an excellent balance of affordability, comfort, and cosmetic improvement. With proper care, they can last several years before requiring maintenance or replacement.

**Patient Aftercare & Warranty Disclaimer:

At our clinic, we are committed to helping you maintain the best possible results from your dental treatment. Whether you have received crowns, implants, fillings, or any other service, proper aftercare is essential to ensure their longevity and function.

Aftercare & Dentist’s Advice
Please follow all aftercare instructions and professional advice provided by your dentist. This includes maintaining good oral hygiene, attending recommended check-ups, and avoiding habits that may put undue stress on your teeth and dental work. For example, you should avoid putting excessive pressure on the teeth, such as biting hard into an uncut apple, ice, hard candy, or using your teeth to open packages.

Warranty & Responsibility
The durability of your treatment depends on your individual habits, oral hygiene, and commitment to follow-up care. Our warranty is conditional upon following your dentist’s advice and attending any scheduled follow-up appointments. Damage caused by neglect, misuse, trauma, or failure to follow professional guidance may not be covered.

Follow-Up Support
We provide ongoing support after your treatment to monitor your oral health and the condition of your restorations. If you notice any discomfort, loosening, or other concerns, please contact us promptly so we can address the issue early.

By proceeding with treatment, you acknowledge the importance of your role in maintaining your dental health and agree to follow the recommended care guidelines.
