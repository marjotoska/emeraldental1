The Basics

What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. It's placed directly into your jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over time. Once healed, it acts as a stable anchor for a crown, bridge, or denture. Unlike removable dentures, implants don't slip or shift when you eat or speak, which is why patients describe them as feeling like natural teeth.

Key takeaway: An implant replaces the tooth root, not just the visible tooth, which is why it feels and functions naturally.

The Process

Your Step-by-Step Treatment Journey

Getting dental implants abroad can feel complicated. It isn't, once you know the sequence. Here's exactly what happens from your first message to us through to your finished smile.

1

Free Online Consultation

You start by sending us photos or X-rays of your teeth, along with your medical history. Our surgeons review your case remotely and give you an initial treatment plan and price estimate before you book any travel. This step costs you nothing and helps you decide with confidence.

2

Booking Your Trip

Once you confirm your treatment plan, we help you plan your visit. This includes suggested flight times that fit your treatment schedule, hotel recommendations near the clinic, and an estimate of how many days you'll need to stay for this specific visit.

Key takeaway: Most first visits require 3 to 5 days on the ground, depending on whether grafting is needed.

3

Arrival and In-Person Exam

On your first day, you'll have a full clinical exam, including 3D imaging of your jaw. If your tooth was extracted recently, this step may be quicker since recent scans are still valid. Your surgeon confirms the final plan face-to-face and answers any remaining questions before surgery.

4

Implant Placement Surgery

The surgery itself is short, around 20 minutes per implant, and less uncomfortable than a tooth extraction. Your surgeon numbs the area with local anaesthetic, opens the gum, and places the titanium post directly into the jawbone. If your jawbone needs reinforcement first, this is handled here too, through bone grafting or a sinus lift.

Key takeaway: The surgical placement takes about 20 minutes per implant, but the full process spans months due to healing.

5

Healing and Osseointegration

After placement, your jawbone needs time to grow around and fuse with the implant. This process is called osseointegration, and it typically takes 6 months. You return home during this period and continue your normal life, with only minor aftercare needed.

6

Crown Placement and Final Fit

Once healing is complete, you return to the clinic for your final visit. We take new impressions of your mouth, place the abutment (the connector piece), and fit your permanent crown. This visit is usually shorter than your first, often 2 to 3 days.

When Extra Care Is Needed

What If Your Jawbone Can't Support an Implant?

If your jawbone is too thin or soft, you may need bone grafting or a sinus lift before implant placement. A bone graft rebuilds a solid base for the implant, since your bite exerts real pressure on the bone. A sinus lift adds bone below the sinus when natural bone has thinned due to missing upper back teeth. Minor grafting can often be done the same day as your implant surgery. More extensive grafting may need its own healing period of a few months before implants are placed.

Key takeaway: Grafting adds time to your treatment plan, but your surgeon will tell you upfront during the online consultation if you need it.

Recovery

Caring for Your Implant After Surgery

Recovery in the days right after surgery matters for how well your implant heals. Avoid rinsing your mouth forcefully, and don't touch the surgical site with your tongue or fingers, since this can dislodge the blood clot and cause bleeding. Skip straws, spitting, and smoking for the same reason.

Stick to soft, cool foods for the first few days and avoid anything spicy, hot, or small enough to get stuck in the gum, like popcorn or seeds. Stay hydrated with cold drinks, and avoid alcohol until the anaesthetic has fully worn off. Most patients return to their normal diet within a week.

Key takeaway: Careful aftercare in the first week protects the blood clot and supports faster, smoother healing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dental implant cost in Albania?

A single dental implant costs around €300 in Albania, compared to €1,500 or more in Italy or the UK. The lower price reflects lower local costs, not a lower-quality implant.

How long do I need to stay in Albania for implants?

Most patients need two separate visits: 3 to 5 days for the surgery, and 2 to 3 days a few months later for the crown fitting. The healing period in between is spent at home.

How long does it take for a dental implant to heal?

Healing, known as osseointegration, generally takes 3 to 4 months. This is how long it takes for your jawbone to fuse fully with the titanium implant post.

Do I need bone grafting before getting an implant?

Only if your jawbone is too thin or soft to support an implant safely. Your surgeon can usually tell during the online photo and X-ray review, before you travel.
